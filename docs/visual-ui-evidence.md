## What the screenshots demonstrate

The screenshots provide visible evidence of:
- separate Admin and Customer entry flows;
- admin authentication and invalid-login feedback;
- dashboard, menu, customer, order, and sales views;
- create forms;
- empty states;
- customer checkout;
- pickup/delivery workflow;
- scheduled ordering;
- success toasts;
- customer order history;
- admin visibility of the resulting order.

## Evidence boundary

These screenshots are visual evidence of the application workflow supplied by the team. They do **not** by themselves prove:
- a public production URL;
- production deployment;
- live production CRUD;
- completion of the full manual QA matrix;
- resolution of P0/P1 bugs;
- completion of the individual oral defense.

Those items still require real runtime/team evidence.

## Suggested presentation use

For the final presentation, select screenshots 1, 4, 6, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15 to show the progression from entry → management → customer ordering → validation → end-to-end order visibility.
